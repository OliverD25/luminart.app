Multi-Monitor & Window Management

Take Full Control of Your Monitors & Windows

Windows 11 forgets your window positions, scrambles your layout when a display sleeps, and hides multiple app windows behind cramped taskbar previews. WinCraft fixes all of it inside a single lightweight process.

WindowDeck — Upgraded Taskbar Previews & Spatial Window Manager (Win+Alt+A)

See all open windows of an app laid out in a clean row above your taskbar—arranged by monitor just like your physical desk, with one-key access (D) to other virtual desktops.

  • Replace Native Taskbar Previews: Optionally swap out Windows 11's default hover thumbnails. Rest your mouse on any taskbar icon with 2+ windows (0.15s) and WindowDeck opens right above it.
  • Supercharge Win+1…9: Optionally bind Win+Number to open WindowDeck for any taskbar button and cycle through its windows while holding Win.
  • Drag or Keyboard-Switch: Drag live window cards to reorder the app's windows on the taskbar, move them to another monitor, or drop them onto another virtual desktop—or press 1–9 to jump to a window, Ctrl+W to close, and F2 to rename.

LayoutKeeper — Reboot-Proof Multi-Monitor Memory (Win+Alt+L)

Remembers which virtual desktop and monitor every window belongs on, the exact order of thumbnails in your taskbar group, and which window is in front—putting everything back after a reboot. Unplugged monitors are respected, and any custom order you set in WindowDeck is saved automatically.

QuickJump — Neovim's Harpoon for Your Windows (Win+Alt+Q)

Pin up to 10 essential windows to digits 0–9 (Win+Alt+Shift+Q + digit) and jump back to any of them from anywhere (Win+Alt+Q + digit). Automatically switches virtual desktops, restores minimized windows without moving them, remembers your pins across restarts, and even launches the app if it's closed.

ScreenDimmer — Hardware DDC/CI & Click-Through Display Control (Win+Alt+F1/F2/F3)

Cover idle monitors with a click-through black overlay that turns translucent under your cursor, or power displays down directly over DDC/CI (Win+Alt+Shift+F1/F2/F3) like a physical power button—without Windows moving your open windows onto your main screen.

WindowNamer — Persistent Custom Window Titles (Win+Alt+E)

Turn three identical browser or terminal windows into "Mail", "Research", and "Bank". Custom names show up in the title bar, Alt+Tab, taskbar thumbnails, and WindowDeck—and stay put even when the app changes its page title or restarts.

The Command Palette (Win+Alt+P)

One Search Box for Everything on Your PC

Summon the palette right on the monitor where your pointer lives. Type a few letters to launch apps, switch windows across virtual desktops, calculate math as you type, or search every drive in milliseconds via Everything.

  • Learns Your Habits: WinCraft remembers what you typed before picking a result. Type the same shorthand again and your favorite match jumps straight to #1.
  • Batch Window Actions: Move a window to the next monitor (Shift+→), or mark multiple windows (Ctrl+Space or Ctrl+A) right in the search list to move, minimize, or close them all at once.
  • Instant Prefix Filters: Type a symbol (or w for windows, a for apps) to focus your search immediately:

Polish, Themes & Hotkey Control

Everyday Ergonomics & Zero Conflicts

  • ShortcutDetector (Win+Alt+S): See every global key combination already claimed on your PC and test shortcuts live before you assign them.
  • Single-Press & 2-Step Chord Hotkeys: Bind any command in the palette (Ctrl+K) or Settings to a standard shortcut or a two-step chord like Win+Alt+K, S. Live status dots tell you immediately if a shortcut is active (🟢) or taken by another app (⚠️).
  • QuickActions — Windows 11 Switches, One Search Away: Type dark, center, or awake in the palette and press Enter to flip the Windows 11 settings that hide several clicks deep. Every change shows a notice with Undo, and Ctrl+K gives any action its own hotkey.
  • LanguageIndicator — Clean On-Screen Input HUD: Switch from Ukrainian to English and get a sleek, click-through translucent panel right in the center of your screen (UK → EN) that fades out smoothly without ever stealing window focus.
  • 5 Native Themes with Live Hover Preview: Choose between Classic, Fluent (native Windows 11 glass that smartly turns solid on battery saver), Terminal, Paper, and High Contrast—each in Light, Dark, or System mode. Hover over any theme tile in Settings to preview it immediately across every open WinCraft window.

Architecture & Trust

Engineered to Respect Your Machine

  • 100% Portable & Admin-Free: One standalone wincraft.exe. Run it portably from any folder, or run wincraft.exe --install to set up a Start Menu shortcut and a user-level startup task—zero administrator privileges required.

  • Isolated, Crash-Proof JSON Configs: Settings live in %LOCALAPPDATA%\WinCraft with one JSON file per plugin. If a manual edit breaks a file, WinCraft safely falls back to defaults without overwriting your file or breaking your other plugins.

  • Self-Healing Updates: Optional hourly update checks verify SHA256 checksums and automatically roll back to your previous executable if a new version fails to run cleanly for 30 seconds.

  • 100% Private (Zero Telemetry): No accounts, no analytics, no background data collection. Your layouts, bookmarks, and usage history never leave your PC.

  • 1-Trait Rust Plugin System: Want to add your own feature in an afternoon? Implement one Rust trait and describe your settings—WinCraft draws the UI, binds the hotkeys, and saves the config automatically.
