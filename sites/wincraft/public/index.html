The Command Palette (Win+Alt+P)

One Search Box for Everything on Your PC

Summon the palette right on the monitor where your cursor lives. Type a few letters to launch apps, jump to open windows across any virtual desktop, search every disk in milliseconds via Everything, or use quick symbol prefixes to take instant action:

PrefixActionWhat It Does
(none)Blended SearchSearches WinCraft commands, installed & Store apps, open windows, bookmarks, and files at once—ranked automatically by what you use most.

Built-In Power Plugins

Five Essential Tools. Zero Extra Processes.

Instead of running five separate background utilities fighting over keyboard hooks and tray icons, WinCraft runs everything inside a unified host.

  • LayoutKeeper — Never Lose Your Window Setup Again

    Remembers which monitor and virtual desktop every window lives on—plus the exact order of thumbnails in your taskbar group. Reboot or unplug a display and restore your entire workspace with Win+Alt+L, or visually drag and reorder windows across desktops using the live Arrange Strip (Win+Alt+A).

  • ScreenDimmer — Hardware & Overlay Monitor Control

    Black out idle monitors with a click-through overlay (Win+Alt+F1/F2/F3) that turns translucent under your cursor, or power displays down directly over DDC/CI (Win+Alt+Shift+F1/F2/F3) without Windows scrambling your open windows onto your main screen.

  • WindowNamer — Persistent Custom Window Titles

    Turn three identical browser windows into "Mail", "Research", and "Bank" (Win+Alt+E). Custom names show up in the title bar, Alt+Tab, and taskbar thumbnails—and persist even when the app changes its page title or restarts.

  • LanguageIndicator — Clean On-Screen Input HUD

    Switch from Ukrainian to English and get a sleek, click-through translucent HUD right in the center of your screen (UK → EN) that fades out smoothly without ever stealing window focus.

  • ShortcutDetector — End Global Hotkey Conflicts

    Press Win+Alt+Q to see every global shortcut already registered on your PC and test key combinations before you assign them.

Why Power Users & Developers Love WinCraft

Engineered to Respect Your Machine

  • Single Portable Binary: Compiles down to one standalone wincraft.exe. No runtime installers, no webview overhead, no administrator privileges required.

  • Crash-Proof Configs: All settings live in clean, human-readable JSON files under %LOCALAPPDATA%\WinCraft. Every plugin gets its own file—if you make a syntax error editing one, WinCraft safely falls back to defaults without wiping your configuration.

  • Self-Healing Updates: Optional daily update checks verify SHA256 checksums and automatically roll back to your previous binary if a new version fails to run cleanly for 30 seconds.

  • 1-Trait Plugin Architecture: Want to build your own tool? Implement a single Rust trait and describe your settings. WinCraft handles all the Win32 plumbing, global hotkeys, config persistence, and UI rendering automatically.
