Multi-Monitor & Window Management

Built for Heavy Multi-Monitor & Virtual Desktop Setups

Stop losing track of where your windows live. WinCraft gives you visual, keyboard-first, and mouse-friendly control over every screen and virtual desktop.

1. WindowDeck — Upgraded Taskbar Previews & Spatial Window Manager (Win+Alt+A)

See all windows of an app laid out in a clean row above the taskbar—organized spatially by monitor just like your physical desk, with quick access (D) to other virtual desktops.

  • Replace Windows 11's Native Taskbar Hover: Optionally swap out Windows' cramped taskbar thumbnails. Hover over any taskbar icon with 2+ windows for 0.15s and WindowDeck opens right above it.
  • Supercharge Win+1…9: Optionally bind Win+Number to open WindowDeck for any taskbar slot and cycle through its windows while holding Win.
  • Drag, Reorder & Act Instantly: Drag live window cards to reorder taskbar buttons, move them to another monitor, or send them to another virtual desktop. Or stay on the keyboard: press 1–9 to jump to a card, Ctrl+W to close, Ctrl+N for a new window, or F2 to rename it.

2. LayoutKeeper — Reboot-Proof Multi-Monitor Memory (Win+Alt+L)

Never rebuild your workspace after a reboot, crash, or monitor disconnect again.

  • Full Spatial & Taskbar Restore: Remembers the exact virtual desktop, physical monitor, taskbar thumbnail order, and active z-order for every open window—and puts them all back automatically after a restart.
  • Smart Display Handling: Unplug a laptop monitor and LayoutKeeper leaves those windows alone until your screen is back. Pair it with WindowDeck and any custom window order you drag into place is automatically saved to your layout.

3. ScreenDimmer — Hardware DDC/CI & Click-Through Display Control (Win+Alt+F1/F2/F3)

Turn off or dim secondary monitors without Windows thinking you unplugged them and dumping all your windows onto your main screen.

  • True Hardware Power-Off (Win+Alt+Shift+F1/F2/F3): Powers down monitors directly over DDC/CI (or drops brightness to 0) while keeping your desktop layout 100% intact and locking the cursor from getting lost on dark screens.
  • Click-Through Black Overlay (Win+Alt+F1/F2/F3): Covers idle monitors in black that turns partially translucent the moment your mouse hovers over it.

4. QuickJump — Neovim's Harpoon for Your Windows (Win+Alt+Q)

Pin up to 10 essential windows to digits 0–9 (Win+Alt+Shift+Q + digit) and teleport to any of them from anywhere (Win+Alt+Q + digit).

  • Cross-Desktop Teleportation: Automatically switches virtual desktops and unminimizes your target window without moving it.
  • Survives Reboots: Remembers your pinned slots across restarts by app and title—and even launches the app for you if it isn't running yet.

5. WindowNamer — Persistent Custom Window Titles (Win+Alt+E)

Turn three identical browser or terminal windows into "Mail", "Research", and "Prod Server". Your custom title shows up in the title bar, Alt+Tab, taskbar previews, and WindowDeck—and stays put even when the web page changes its title or the app restarts.

The Command Palette (Win+Alt+P)

One Search Box on the Monitor Where You're Working

Summon the palette right on the screen where your pointer lives. Launch apps, switch windows across virtual desktops, calculate math as you type (200 * 15%), or search every drive in milliseconds via Everything.

Polish, Themes & Hotkey Control

Everyday Ergonomics & Zero Conflicts

  • ShortcutDetector (Win+Alt+S): See every global shortcut already claimed on your PC and test key combinations live before you assign them.
  • Single-Press & 2-Step Chord Shortcuts: Bind any WinCraft action (Ctrl+K) to a standard hotkey or a two-step chord like Win+Alt+K, S with live conflict status indicators.
  • LanguageIndicator: Switch input languages (e.g., UK → EN) and get a sleek, click-through translucent HUD in the center of your monitor that fades out without ever stealing window focus.
  • 5 Native Themes with Live Hover Preview: Choose between Classic, Fluent (Windows 11 glass), Terminal, Paper, and High Contrast in Light, Dark, or System mode.

Architecture & Trust

Engineered to Respect Your Machine

  • 100% Portable & Admin-Free: One standalone wincraft.exe. Run it portably, or run wincraft.exe --install to add a Start Menu shortcut and user-level startup task—zero administrator rights required.

  • Crash-Proof Per-Plugin JSON Configs: Every plugin stores its settings in its own file under %LOCALAPPDATA%\WinCraft. A broken manual edit affects only that plugin and is never overwritten.

  • Self-Healing Updates: Optional daily update checks verify SHA256 checksums and automatically roll back to your previous binary if an update fails to run cleanly for 30 seconds.

  • 100% Private (Zero Telemetry): No analytics, no accounts, no cloud sync. Everything stays on your machine.
