## Arrange strip

### Stand-in cards can be dragged

The faded cards for windows on other monitors now move like cards.

- Drag one within the main column to change the taskbar position of that window. The window stays on its own monitor.
- Drag one into the column of another monitor. The window moves there, like its real card.
- With a stand-in selected, Ctrl+←→ or Alt+←→ changes its position. Ctrl+Shift+arrows move the window to another monitor or desktop.

## Settings

### Themes have their own page

The themes now have their own Settings page, right after General.
