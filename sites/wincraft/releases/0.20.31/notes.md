## WindowDeck

### Opens in one step

WindowDeck now appears in one step. The short flash at the wrong place is gone.

### Windows' own thumbnails stay away

The thumbnail strip of Windows stays away while WindowDeck is open. This is also true when you press Win+digit again.

### Hover waits for the mouse

A card under a cursor that did not move is not previewed. After you move the mouse, the card you point at becomes the one selection.

### New keys

- **Ctrl+W** closes the selected window. The app may still ask to save.
- **Ctrl+N** opens a new window of the app and switches to it.

## Command palette

### Folder search

After you press Tab on a folder, typing also finds files and folders in its subfolders.
