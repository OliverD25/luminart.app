## Arrange strip

### Open the strip by taskbar number

Press Win+Alt+A, then a digit. The strip opens above taskbar button N, with window N selected. Then the arrow keys move between cards, Enter opens one, and Esc closes the strip.

- The digits 1 to 9 work, and 0 means 10.
- With more than 10 buttons, type two digits quickly. For example, 1 and then 1 means 11.
- You can release the keys before the digit, or keep holding Win+Alt.
- While the strip is open, WinCraft takes Win+Alt+digit instead of the jump list of Windows.
- You can change the Win+Alt+A part in Settings.

This follows Principle 4 of the project, Keyboard first. Every feature can be reached from the keyboard.
