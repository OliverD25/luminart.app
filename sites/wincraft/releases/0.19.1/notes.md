## Arrange strip

### Store apps

Apps installed from the Microsoft Store, such as Claude, now get the Arrange strip when you rest the mouse on their taskbar button. Win+Alt+A also opens on them.

## Command palette

### One command to update

Type `upd` and pick **Update WinCraft**. It checks now and installs a newer version at once. If there is no newer version, it says that WinCraft is up to date.
