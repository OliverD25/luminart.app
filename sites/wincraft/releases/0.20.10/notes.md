## Fixes

### No wait after the digit

Win+Alt+A, then a digit, now opens the strip at once. If you type a second digit quickly, for example for button 12, the strip jumps to that button.

### Arrow keys land on stand-in cards

The faded cards for windows on other monitors now get the selection like normal cards. Enter opens the real window. A click on a stand-in selects it too.
