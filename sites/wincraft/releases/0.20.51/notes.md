## WindowDeck

### Win+1, let go, Win+2 works again

This works again when the keyboard hook of another program keeps the Win release from WinCraft.

WindowDeck also no longer fights with Windows over the keyboard after Win and a digit. It takes the keyboard back once, not every second.
