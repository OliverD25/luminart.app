## ShortcutDetector

### In the WinCraft look

The shortcut detector (Win+Alt+S) is rebuilt in the own style and themes of WinCraft.

- It has a search box.
- It has the filter buttons All, Ctrl, Alt, Shift and Win.
- It has a **Show free** switch.
- The Check a shortcut row shows the pressed keys and the answer on one line.
- Short coloured tags show the status: Windows, App, WinCraft or Free.
- The column headers stay at the top while the list scrolls.

## Settings

### Round glass corners

In the Fluent theme, the corners of the palette are now one clean curve. Before, small square corners showed around the rounded ones.

## Internal

Line coverage is back over 70 % with 84 new tests. The biggest source files are split into smaller files.
