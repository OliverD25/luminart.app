## WindowDeck

### Opening time in the log

Each time WindowDeck opens, it now writes one line to the WinCraft log with the time of each step. This happens when you open it with Win+digit, with Win+Alt+A and then a digit, or by resting the mouse on a taskbar button.

Nothing else changes. The numbers decide the next steps to make WindowDeck open faster.
