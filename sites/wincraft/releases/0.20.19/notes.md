## Fixes

### Card badges no longer overlap

In the Arrange strip, the badges "Same title" and "Moved" now sit on the top-right corner of the window picture. They are never in the title row.

- On a narrow card, they become small icons. A copy icon means Same title, and arrows mean Moved. Hover for the full text.
- The picture makes room for them, so they stay readable over the live window picture.
- "Moved" now has a tooltip that explains it.
