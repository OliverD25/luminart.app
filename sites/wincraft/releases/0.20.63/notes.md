## Detailed log

### A toggle row in the palette

The Detailed log is now a toggle row in the palette, like the QuickActions switches. It has a red recording tile, the title "Detailed log", a line that says what it does now, and a switch. Enter flips it, and the palette stays open.

## Fixes

### LOG tag

The LOG tag in the footer of the palette no longer covers the WinCraft name.
