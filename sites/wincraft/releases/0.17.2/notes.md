## Updates

### A progress bar for the update

The only sign of a running update was a small "Downloading… 42%" next to Check for updates, far from the Update now button you had just clicked. Now a bar under the release row on the About page shows the two steps, download and install. When the server gives no file size, a sliding segment shows that the update is moving.
