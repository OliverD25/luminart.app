## Settings

### Themes

Settings → General → Appearance now has theme tiles. Each tile has a small preview.

- **Classic** is the look you know, unchanged. It stays the default.
- **Terminal** uses a monospace font, square corners and tighter rows.
- **Paper** uses serif fonts (Lora and Cormorant, now included in WinCraft) and thin lines.
- **High contrast** is black or white with strong borders. WinCraft uses it by itself when Windows high contrast is on.

Rest the mouse on a tile, or move to it with the arrow keys. The whole app then shows that theme. Move away and it goes back. Click to choose.

The mode (System, Dark, Light) is now a separate control under the tiles. Your old choice is kept.

If an update fails and WinCraft goes back to an older version, that version cannot read the new theme setting. It then uses its defaults until you update again.

A click on a stand-in card now opens its window, like a click on the real card.
