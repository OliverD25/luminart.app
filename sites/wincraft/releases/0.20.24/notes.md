## Settings

### Hotkeys have their own page

"Keyboard shortcuts" in Settings is now called **Hotkeys**. It is the one place for everything about hotkeys.

- A green dot means the hotkey works.
- A warning triangle means another app or a WinCraft command holds the keys. Hover to see which.
- A grey dot means its plugin is off.
- A line at the top sums it up, for example "12 working · 1 taken by another app".
- The hotkey list left the About page.

### Fixed on that page

- The plugin icon no longer overlaps the group name.
- "What is a chord?" is no longer cut off.
- The search box no longer touches the "Search by keys" button.
