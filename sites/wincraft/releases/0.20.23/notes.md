## Updates

### WinCraft opens again where you left off

Start the update from the palette, and the palette opens again after the restart. Start it from Settings → About, and About opens again. An automatic update or a tray update opens nothing.

This works from the next update on (0.20.23 to 0.20.24), because the old version starts the update.

## Settings

### The Fluent tile shows its glass

On the Themes page, the Fluent preview now shows a colourful desktop through a half see-through window.
