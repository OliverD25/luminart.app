## Arrange strip

### Win+Alt+A waits for a taskbar number

Number badges appear above the taskbar buttons for 3 seconds. A WinCraft line shows at the bottom.

- Press a digit. The strip opens above that button, with that window selected.
- Press Win+Alt+A again. The strip opens for the app in front.
- Press Esc or another key, click, or wait 3 seconds with no key. Then nothing happens.

### Arrow keys in every strip

The ←→↑↓ keys move the selection, and Enter opens the window.

- Ctrl+←→ moves a window within its monitor.
- Ctrl+Shift+←→ moves it to the next monitor.
- Ctrl+Shift+↑↓ moves it to the next desktop.
- Alt+←→ still changes its taskbar number.
