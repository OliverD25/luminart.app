## Settings

### About page

Updates now sits right under Version. One button follows the state: **Check for updates**, **Update now**, and **Cancel** while a download runs. Cancel removes the partial file, and your version stays. The release notes open right below it. Links and Fonts moved to the end.

### No more wrapped buttons

Buttons, links and the version line stay on one line. The descriptions on the left get narrower instead.
