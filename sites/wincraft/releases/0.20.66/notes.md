## Command palette

### Move the palette

Press and hold on any blank part of the palette, then move the mouse. The palette moves.

It opens there again on that monitor, also after a restart. Each monitor remembers its own place.

### Move it back

Double-click a blank part, or run **Palette: Move back to the centre**, to put the palette back.
