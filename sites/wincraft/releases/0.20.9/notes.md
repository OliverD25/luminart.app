## Arrange strip

### The hover strip opens faster

The strip now waits 0.15 seconds on a taskbar button. Before, it waited 0.4 seconds. In Settings, LayoutKeeper, **Hover delay** can now go down to 0.05 seconds. If you never changed the delay, you get the new value by itself.
