## Detailed log

### What it is

The Detailed log is for finding bugs. It is off by default and costs nothing while off.

Turn it on from the palette ("Detailed log: Turn on/off"), from the tray menu, or in Settings → General.

### What it records

While on, it records keys, the window in front, slow moments and WindowDeck steps. It writes them into `%LOCALAPPDATA%\WinCraft\detailed\`. The tray tooltip and a LOG tag in the palette show that it is on.

### Commands

- **Detailed log: Mark this moment** notes when a bug happened.
- **Save a report** puts the logs into one folder.
- **Delete the files** removes them.

The files stay on your PC. They hold window titles and palette text.
