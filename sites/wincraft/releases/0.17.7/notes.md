## Settings

### Smooth switches

An on/off switch now shows your click at once and slides with an easing curve. Before, it could slide back and forth for a moment while the setting was being saved.

Choices and dropdowns in Settings also show your pick at once. The settings window now redraws as soon as a setting changes.
