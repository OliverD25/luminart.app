## WindowDeck

### A better fix for dragging

In the **By monitor** view, dragging a card no longer pushes cards onto a hidden second line. The empty monitor columns ("Drop a window here") now open only as far as there is free room.

This also undoes 0.20.46, which made the window one row taller to show that line.
