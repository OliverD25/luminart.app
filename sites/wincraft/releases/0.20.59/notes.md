## WindowDeck

### Reach cards 11 to 20

In an open WindowDeck, Shift and a digit opens the card ten higher. Shift+1 is card 11, and Shift+0 is card 20.

- Plain digits work as before. The digits 1 to 9 work, and 0 is 10.
- Ctrl and a digit still jump to a taskbar button.
- When the desktop has more than ten cards, the footer shows the new key.
