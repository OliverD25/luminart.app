## Updates

### The palette shows when an update is ready

When WinCraft knows about a newer version, the "Update WinCraft" row shows a badge such as "0.20.21 available". It says "Installs WinCraft 0.20.21 now". It also appears in the WinCraft group without typing. The badge appears as soon as the daily check finds a release.
