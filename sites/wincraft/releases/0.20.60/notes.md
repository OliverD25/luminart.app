## Updates

### Update ready in the palette

WinCraft now tells you about a new version in the palette, and finds it within the hour.

- An "Update ready" row shows above Recent when a new version is waiting.
- Enter still opens your top recent item. Press Up to select the update, then Enter to install.
- The update panel shows the old and new version, what is new, and **Open release page** (Ctrl+O).
- Ctrl+Del on the row hides it until the next version.

### When WinCraft checks

WinCraft checks every hour, after the PC wakes, and when you open the palette after 15 minutes. Nothing changes while the palette is open.

### Download updates ahead

The new setting **Download updates ahead** is on by default. It downloads and checks the new version in the background. It never does this on a metered connection. So Update takes seconds. It never installs on its own.
