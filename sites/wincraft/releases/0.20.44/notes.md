## QuickActions

### Pick from a list

Some actions now open a short list right in the palette. Type to filter, press Enter to pick, and press Esc to go back.

- **Developer: Kill Process on Port** lists every listening port with its program. Ending one needs Enter twice.
- **Audio: Switch Output Device** lists your speakers and headphones. The current one is marked. There is an Undo.
- **Window: Resize…** lists the standard sizes and your own sizes. Your own sizes are in the setting "More sizes for Window: Resize…".
- **System: Eject USB Drive** lists removable drives. It ejects the drive safely, or shows a notice if the drive is in use.
- **Display: Keep Awake for…** offers 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours or until off. The tray shows the end time.

Other plugins can use the same lists.
