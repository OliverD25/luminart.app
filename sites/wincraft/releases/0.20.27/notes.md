## WindowDeck

### Works without LayoutKeeper

WindowDeck now lists the windows itself when LayoutKeeper is off. With LayoutKeeper on, nothing changes.

## Settings

### Plugin icons

Every plugin now has its own icon on the Plugins and Plugin Store tiles and on the Hotkeys page.

## Command palette

### History is kept

The commands that moved from LayoutKeeper to WindowDeck keep their ranking in the palette.

## Internal

Unused code was removed, and the test coverage floor goes up to 70 %.
