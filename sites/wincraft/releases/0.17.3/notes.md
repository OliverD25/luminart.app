## Arrange strip

### No jump after a drop

When you drop a window card on another monitor or desktop, it now stays where you put it. Before, the card jumped to another place for a moment while WinCraft was still moving the window.

### See the window while you drag

In the By monitor view, a live and slightly see-through picture of the window now follows the mouse. The old place of the card stays faded, so you can see where it came from.
