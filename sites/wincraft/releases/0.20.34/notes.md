## WindowDeck

### F2 renames a window

Press F2 in WindowDeck. The title of the selected card becomes a text box. Enter saves, and Esc or a click elsewhere cancels.

The name belongs to WindowNamer, so it survives restarts. An empty name removes it. WindowNamer must be on.
