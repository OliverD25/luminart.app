## Settings

### Fluent: Windows 11 glass behind the palette

In the Fluent theme, the command palette now shows the frosted glass of Windows 11, with the own rounded corners of Windows.

- It turns solid by itself on battery saver.
- It turns solid when the Windows setting "Transparency effects" is off.
- It turns solid on Windows older than 11 22H2.
- Settings and the Arrange strip stay solid. The other themes are unchanged.
