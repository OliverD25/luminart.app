## Command palette

### New palette with an inspector

The command palette (Win+Alt+P) is bigger now, at 900 × 520. It has an inspector on the right. The inspector shows the details of the selected item and every action with its keys. Press → to walk into the actions.

For windows, the inspector shows:

- a live preview,
- the process, monitor, desktop and memory,
- a "Show on all desktops" switch.

### Mark several windows

Mark windows with Ctrl+Space, with Shift+click, or with Ctrl+A for all. Then you can arrange, minimise, move or close them together. Esc clears the marks first.

### Scopes

Type a prefix to search in one place only. The prefix becomes a chip.

- `w ` for windows
- `a ` for apps
- `! ` for commands
- `= ` for the calculator

### Smaller improvements

- The selected row shows what Enter does, for example "Switch ↵".
- The calculator answers while you type, for example `12*3`. Enter copies the answer. Ctrl+Enter pastes it into the window you came from.
- The palette learns from your picks and marks the top result as "often picked". Ctrl+Del forgets a pick.
- Settings moved to a gear button in the footer (Ctrl+,). The hints in the footer follow the selected item.

### No results

When nothing matches, "Try instead" offers **Ask AI** and **Search the web**. Ask AI opens the AI site of your choice in the browser with your question. Claude is the default. Choose the site in Settings → General.
