## Settings

### Fluent theme

The Fluent theme follows the style of Windows 11.

- Corners are rounder.
- The palette and the plugin tiles have colored icon tiles.
- The selected row has a short accent pill.
- The search field has an accent line under it.

Choose it in Settings → General → Appearance.

The see-through glass of the design is not in this version yet. For now, Fluent uses the same colors on a solid background.
